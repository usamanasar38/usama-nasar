   motion; 200% zoom; sound toggle persists.
5. SEO/LLM: titles, descriptions, canonicals unique per page; JSON-LD valid; sitemap, RSS, robots,
   llms.txt, llms-full.txt and /blog/<slug>.md served with the right content types; no draft or
